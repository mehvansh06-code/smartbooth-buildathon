SmartBooth Autonomous Electoral Operations & Field Cockpit

Domain: build.smartbooth.in • Engine: Gemini 2.5 Flash + PostgreSQL 16

Open Live Demo Cockpit →
01

What Is This Pipeline?

SmartBooth Core is the mission-critical operations cockpit for Indian democratic elections. It eliminates the manual chaos of electoral voter lists by converting raw, unindexed PDF rolls into an intelligent, structured multi-tenant database.

Candidates and booth karyakartas can search, cluster, dispatch slips, and query campaign statistics using natural conversational voice and text without any specialized technical training.

02

Main Function & Purpose

  • • Gruhastha Household Clustering: Resolves house numbers & family heads into cohesive voter units.
  • • Phonetic Devanagari Search: Tolerates colloquial pronunciation shifts ($w \leftrightarrow v, s \leftrightarrow sh, ee \leftrightarrow i$).
  • • Text-to-SQL Analytics: Translates Hindi questions into PostgreSQL queries via Gemini 2.5 Flash.
  • • Instant Slip Dispatch: WhatsApp digital cards & Bluetooth 58mm POS thermal slips.
03

How It Works (Under the Hood)

When a user asks: "सराना में कितने 60+ वरिष्ठ नागरिक अभी वोट नहीं डाले हैं?"

  1. User input passes through tenancy & scope resolvers.
  2. Gemini 2.5 Flash inspects verified PostgreSQL schema and drafts safe parameterized SQL.
  3. Query executes on tenant partition (voters_t5) in ~45ms.
  4. Synthesizes natural Devanagari conversational response with actionable follow-up buttons.

01

What Is This Pipeline?

video.smartbooth.in (Our AI Campaign Video Engine) is a high-speed video rendering system built to deliver 1-to-1 personal campaign appeals from political candidates to individual voters at grassroots scale.

Instead of expensive, slow full-body AI re-renders that hallucinate or look uncanny, this pipeline uses an authentic candidate master recording and splices personalized Devanagari audio & graphics in seconds.

02

Main Function & Purpose

  • • Individual Voter Recognition: Candidate warmly calls the voter by their exact Devanagari name (*"रामलाल जी", "सुनीता देवी"*).
  • • Devanagari Honorific Badges: Dynamic graphic overlay displays voter serial, ward, and booth details.
  • • Ballot Symbol Banner: Persistent high-contrast election symbol banner (*"प्रेस चुनाव चिन्ह #1 - अलमारी"*).
  • • WhatsApp Optimization: Enforces 720x1280 @ 30fps H.264 <3.3MB with +faststart.
03

How It Works (Under the Hood)

Rather than re-generating an entire 30-second video per voter, our Surgical Splice Architecture works in 4 coordinated steps:

  1. ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 generates candidate-cloned personalized greeting audio.
  2. Acoustic cut stitches intro greeting seamlessly into authentic candidate speech body at 3.700s.
  3. Pillow engine renders custom typography overlay with candidate ballot symbol.
  4. FFmpeg stitches video, audio, and graphics into WhatsApp-ready MP4.

Real-Time Multimodal Voice Agent (Conversational Telephony)

Domain: voiceagent.smartbooth.in • Engine: Google Cloud Gemini Live API

Open Voice Portal Test Mic Live
01

What Is This Pipeline?

voiceagent.smartbooth.in is an interactive, real-time Speech-to-Speech conversational voice agent for mass citizen feedback and ward grievance collection.

Unlike static robocalls that blast a pre-recorded monologue, this agent engages citizens in natural two-way conversation, listens empathetically to ground grievances, and files complaints into the campaign database.

02

Main Function & Purpose

  • • Sub-Second Response Latency (~300–600ms): Direct speech-to-speech audio streaming over WebSockets without cascaded STT $\rightarrow$ LLM $\rightarrow$ TTS delays.
  • • Natural Barge-In & Interruption: Voters can speak over the agent naturally; audio buffers clear instantly without robotic overlap.
  • • Colloquial Hinglish Understanding: Handles everyday citizen phrases (*"bijli, paani, naali, sadak, kisan samman"*).
  • • Autonomous Tool Calling: Calls record_voter_issue to record ward complaints directly into PostgreSQL.
03

How It Works (Under the Hood)

The voice pipeline connects standard telephone lines (PSTN) and browser microphones directly to Google Cloud Vertex AI:

  1. Twilio / Exotel streams 8kHz G.711 μ-law audio via WebSockets to FastAPI bridge server.
  2. Audio Transcoder upsamples audio to 16kHz PCM and streams to Gemini Live Multimodal session.
  3. Gemini Live processes tone and intent, generating 24kHz PCM voice response.
  4. Transcoder downsamples to 8kHz μ-law back to caller with sub-second turnaround.

Unified Tripartite Ecosystem

How the Three Pipelines Amplify Each Other

The three pipelines are not isolated experiments—they form a closed-loop autonomous campaign flywheel.

PIPELINE 1 ➔ PIPELINE 2

Electoral Roll to Video Personalization

Pipeline 1 extracts Devanagari voter names, honorific titles, and booth numbers, feeding clean structured data directly into Pipeline 2's video rendering queue.

PIPELINE 2 ➔ PIPELINE 3

Video Outreach to Voice Grievance Call

After personalized WhatsApp campaign videos are delivered, Pipeline 3 triggers empathetic two-way voice calls asking if the citizen has local civic concerns.

PIPELINE 3 ➔ PIPELINE 1

Grievances Back to War Room Intelligence

When Pipeline 3 collects civic complaints (*"Ward 3 water pipeline broken"*), it auto-executes database tools that update Pipeline 1's war room dashboard in real time.
